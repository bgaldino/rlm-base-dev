Required Demo Setup Steps

  1. Refresh the Decision Tables – use the decision table refresh widget directly below – ensure the toggle is off & say "Full Refresh" directly below the toggle. Click the "Next" button and then the "Finish" button.
  2. From the Setup Menu use the Quick Find to search for "Product Discovery Settings"
  3. Scroll to the "Select Default Catalog", deselect the existing catalog and select the newly created catalog. The new catalog will be named after the company the agent researched.
  4. Under the "Search Settings" section, click the "Create Full Index" button. On the next screen, click the "Rebuild Index" from the modal select "Full Index Rebuild" then click the "Rebuild" button. **This process can take up to 15 minutes, refresh the screen to see status updates.**
  5. After the index rebuild is complete, verify the pricing & catalog index were successful.
    1. From the "Quotes" create a new quote – only needs a generic name
    2. From the Quote record, click the "Browse Catalogs" button
      1. You should see your company's catalog and all of the products and pricing that the agent skill created.
      2. Delete the quote record
  6. From the "Products" tab
    1. Create a filter for the "All Products" list view
      1. Create Date = TODAY
    2. Select 3-4 products that you want displayed on the Experience site.
      1. From the product record select the "RLM Display In Self Service" checkbox under the Digital Experience Site section.
  7. From the "Accounts" tab
    1. Select the "New This Week" list view and pin it.
