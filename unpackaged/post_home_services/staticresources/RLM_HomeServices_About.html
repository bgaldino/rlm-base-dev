Everything you need to know about the Home Services RCA demo

Due to the variability of potential companies this demo will be utilized for, a strategic decision was made to not pre-populate any product/services data. Instead, an AI SKILL (Cursor or Claude Code) has been developed to research your prospect company, create services & pricing and load all necessary data.

Follow the tabs at the top of the page, left to right to finalize your demo setup and be demo ready in as little as 30 minutes.

This demo flow is designed to be representative of a typical home services provider (Alarm companies, Pest Control, House Cleaning, ect) where quotes and contracts are typically provided in-person. Speed and efficiency are critical to maintaining deal velocity as the average acceptance (or “closing”) rate for in-person home services quotes typically ranges between 30% to 50%.

Factors that Influence Acceptance Rates

  • Responsiveness & Speed: Research from HomeAdvisor shows that 78% of homeowners hire the first contractor who responds to them.
  • Pricing Transparency: Homeowners are increasingly willing to pay more for pros with excellent reputations and transparent operations.

The Demo

  • Prospective customer expresses interest via a signup form (Experience Cloud)
  • Account, Opportunity, Quote and Contract records have a simplistic layout that is easy to view on an ipad or similar mobile device.
  • Services are priced on a “Monthly” basis
  • Billing is “Milestone” so Invoices are only generated once the service has been performed and recorded (Field Service App)
