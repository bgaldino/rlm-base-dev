# Internal and External Reviewer Statuses in Salesforce Contracts

Salesforce Contracts supports various reviewer statuses to enable contract authors to monitor and evaluate document review progress.

REVIEWER STATUS	DESCRIPTION
Not Initiated	The reviewer is yet to receive the email.
Not Started	The reviewer is yet to click the link in the email to start the review process.
In Progress	The reviewer is reviewing the document.
Completed	The reviewer has clicked the Mark as Complete button to confirm the completion of the review.
