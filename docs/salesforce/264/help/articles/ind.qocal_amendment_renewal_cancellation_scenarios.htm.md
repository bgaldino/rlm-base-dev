# Amendment, Renewal, and Cancellation Scenarios

Explore complete amendment, renewal, and cancellation examples from the initial asset state through order decomposition, fulfillment, and billing.

REQUIRED EDITIONS
Available in: Lightning Experience
Available in: Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer Editions of Revenue Cloud where Transaction Management is enabled

These example scenarios are for admins and business users who want to understand how amendments, renewals, and cancellations flow end-to-end across Revenue Cloud. Each scenario traces a single lifecycle action from start to finish. You learn how the asset changes, what quotes, orders, and order products are created, how decomposition and fulfillment process the request, and the resulting billing and asset states.

Unlike setup documentation, which explains how to configure each feature independently, these scenarios show how configured features work together during real business operations.

Prerequisites

To use these example scenarios, you need:

An org with Revenue Cloud configured with a complete product catalog, pricing, order orchestration, and billing components.
A working knowledge of how quotes, orders, assets, order fulfillment, and billing operate in Revenue Cloud.

To build or refresh this knowledge, complete these Trailhead badges:

Efficient Sales with Revenue Cloud
Asset Lifecycle Management with Revenue Cloud
Billing Basics in Revenue Cloud

These examples cover common amendment, renewal, and cancellation scenarios. Adapt and expand them to meet your company's needs.

Quantity Amendment Example
Explore a quantity increase (upsell) to see how the updated asset, fulfillment, and billing schedule reflect the change.
Subscription Renewal Example
Trace an asset renewal to see how a new subscription term is established.
Asset Cancellation Example
Trace an asset cancellation to see how the asset is retired and billing stops.
Asset Swap Example
Trace a product swap to see how Revenue Cloud replaces one lifecycle-managed asset with another and orchestrates both sides of the change.
