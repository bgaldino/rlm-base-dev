# Contracts AI

Use generative AI to change how your business handles contracts, clauses, and documents. Contracts AI streamlines the labor-intensive process of drafting clauses for legal agreements and enables the efficient extraction of contract details and clauses from complex PDFs. Einstein boosts your productivity, accuracy, and compliance in the digital age.

Effortlessly Digitalize Legacy Contracts and Create Contract Records with Contracts AI
Use generative AI to streamline and automate the process of digitizing PDF contracts, and significantly reduce the time and effort required to identify and extract data such as contract fields and clauses. Contracts AI helps minimize the risk of errors that occur during manual data entry. Track and review multiple extractions from a comprehensive user interface.
Automate Clause Generation and Quickly Modify Clauses with Contracts AI
Use Contracts AI to draft legal clauses based on input parameters or user prompts. Automate clause drafting to reduce manual research, minimize stakeholder collaboration, and enhance consistency in language and format.
Instantly Draft Clauses in Microsoft 365 Word Add-in with Contracts AI
Use Contracts AI to draft and insert legal clauses directly in the Microsoft 365 editor based on input parameters or user queries. Accelerate clause creation for document templates and contract documents, and add reusable clauses to the clause library.
