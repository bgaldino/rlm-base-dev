# Salesforce Contracts Analytics Dashboards

Salesforce Contracts Analytics brings in console-based dashboards and embedded analytics to provide a comprehensive overview and analysis of your contracts from the creation to negotiation and approval stages.

The personalized dashboards and analytics for contract administrators, sales managers, and contract users provide insights to track and monitor contracts in different stages, identify the contracts that need attention, and analyze the contracts based on revenue.

Contract Lifecycle Analytics for Contract Administrators
The Contract Lifecycle Analytics dashboard for contract administrators provides a comprehensive analysis of contracts based on status, completion time, renewal date, and clause to quickly identify the areas that need more attention.
Contract Lifecycle Analytics for Sales Operations
The Contract Lifecycle Analytics dashboard for sales operations provides the sales managers insights to analyze the contracts in different stages, identify top sales contract lines by value, and prioritize the contracts based on renewal date.
Embedded Analytics for Salesforce Contracts
Salesforce Contracts Analytics includes dashboards to embed and access in Lightning Experience pages.
Key Performance Indicators in Salesforce Contracts Analytics
Learn the definitions and calculations of key metrics shown in Salesforce Contracts Analytics.
