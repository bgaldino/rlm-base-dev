# B2B Sales Home Dashboard for Salesforce Contracts

Embed the dashboard on the B2B Sales Home page to get a quick view on the top contracts for renewal based on the contract value. You can filter the contracts that are up for renewal based on the days left before expiration.

Use the B2B Sales Home dashboard to view:

The contracts that are the top priorities for renewal based on their contract value.

The high-value contracts that are due for renewal soon.

The contracts that have the shortest time left before expiration and require immediate attention for renewal.

Patterns or trends in contract value and renewal timelines.
