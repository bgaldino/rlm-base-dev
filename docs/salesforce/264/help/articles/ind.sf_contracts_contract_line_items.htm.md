# Contract Line Items

Embed this dashboard on the Contract page to view the average revenue of contract line items and compare them with the average price range and average revenue across all accounts.

Use the Contract Line Items dashboard to view:

The average revenue of contract line items compares to the average revenue across all accounts.

The contract line items with significantly higher or lower prices compared to the average price range.

The accounts that contribute the most to the overall average revenue of contract line items.

Patterns or discrepancies in the pricing and revenue of contract line items when compared to the averages across all accounts.
