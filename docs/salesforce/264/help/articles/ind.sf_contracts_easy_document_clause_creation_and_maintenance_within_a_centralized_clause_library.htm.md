# Create and Maintain Document Clauses in a Centralized Library

The Document Clause Library is a central repository of pre-approved and pre-built standardized legal content. Use approved clauses to create document templates and generate legal documents such as contracts. By using the Clause Library, the sales team can create contracts without involving the legal team at every step. The library consolidates and stores all legal language in one place, speeding up the document creation process.

Clause designers can create variations of clauses in multiple languages, categorize them by business unit or product, and manage the clause lifecycle.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, Professional, and Developer editions.

Who: To create and manage document clause sets and document clauses, users need ClauseDesignerUser permission. To use clauses within document templates and documents, users need ClauseUser or ClauseDigitalExperienceUser permissions.

Why: Document Clause Library helps businesses in these ways:

Maintain a clause lifecycle that includes draft, active, approved, and archived.​

Simplify the contract review and negotiation time with pre-approved clauses.​

​Reduce contract and document template creation time.​

​​Reuse standard pre-approved clauses across contracts and document templates.​

​Enhance customer experience and satisfaction through process digitization.​

​​Reduce administrative overheads.​

​Improve compliance management by reducing financial and legal risks.​
