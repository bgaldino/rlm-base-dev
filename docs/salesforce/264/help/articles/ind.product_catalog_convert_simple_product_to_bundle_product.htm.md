# Convert Simple Product to Bundle Product

Change a product’s type to a bundle to update the product definition with bundle-specific features without making duplicates.

REQUIRED EDITIONS
View supported products and editions.
USER PERMISSIONS
NEEDED
To convert a product to a bundle:	Manage Product Catalog
From the Product Catalog Management app’s home page, click Products.
On the product list view page, select the product that you want to convert to a bundle.
Click Edit.
In the Edit Product window, verify that the current product type is Null, and select Bundle as the product type. This change can't be undone. It can affect this product's configuration and related quotes, orders, and assets.
Click Save.
