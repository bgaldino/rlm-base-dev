# Limitations for Contracts AI

Generative AI sometimes produces clauses resembling user queries, encounters difficulties in detecting bias and irrelevant content, and lacks consistent adherence to standard clause formats.

Generative AI sometimes drafts clauses that only replicate the user queries or prompts used as input for generating the clause. It can result in inaccurate and redundant clause output generation. User input example: Provide a clause outlining the parties' responsibilities if there are product recalls.

The generative AI clauses drafted directly from user queries or prompts don't conform to standard clause formats. However, when queries included prerequisites or explanatory context, the output quality improved. User input example: Give a non-solicitation clause for the agreement. The clause must define the scope of the non-solicitation, specifying the types of solicitation activities that are restricted. Determine the timeframe for which the non-solicitation clause will be in effect after the termination of the employment or business relationship. Justify the need for the non-solicitation clause to protect the company's legitimate business interests, trade secrets, or customer relationships.
