# Configuration of Obligation Notifications

The CLM admin can notify your users with timely email and in-app notifications for obligations. In-app notifications are enabled by default, and you can disable email notifications. The notifications trigger when an obligation is assigned or deleted or when the obligation status or metadata is updated.

Obligation Management supports these types of notifications:

Use the Contractual Obligations notification type to send notifications when the state of obligations associated with a contract is updated due to a change in the contract’s status.

Use the Obligation Alerts notification type to send notifications when the obligation personas (manager and assignee) perform actions such as create, delete, and update an obligation.

Manage Notification Delivery Settings for Obligations
The CLM admin can enable or disable obligation email and in-app notifications from Notification Delivery Settings. The notification is sent for all obligation status changes.
