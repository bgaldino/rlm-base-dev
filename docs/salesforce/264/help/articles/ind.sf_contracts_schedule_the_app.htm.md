# Schedule the Salesforce Contracts Analytics App

Select a time outside normal work hours so the data refresh doesn’t interrupt business activities.

To schedule your app, see Schedule Data Refresh for a CRM Analytics App. The app now syncs and runs the recipes in correct order every day at the time you set.
