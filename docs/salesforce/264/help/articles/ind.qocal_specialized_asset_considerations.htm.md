# Specialized Asset Considerations

Revenue Cloud allows you to modify assets with scheduled future transactions, such as upsells, renewals, or swaps, by using specific amendment types. Sales reps can meet precise contract requirements and manage global subscriptions by using exact time zone precision to establish accurate asset state periods. Additionally, users can negotiate and track changes to catalog or negotiated rates and grants through an asset-level audit trail during amendments and renewals

Usage-Based Assets
Manage rates, grants, and quantities throughout a usage-based asset's lifecycle. Choose whether renewal quotes carry forward negotiated terms or use current catalog values.
Future-Dated Changes
To adjust assets that have scheduled future transactions, such as upsells, downsells, renewals, transfers, swaps, or attribute changes, use the Managed Asset viewer. This process updates assets that have more than one subsequent future-dated change recorded as an asset state period (ASP).
Backdate Asset Transactions
Apply an amendment, renewal, or cancellation to an asset with a past effective date so that the asset reflects the change from the date it took effect.
