# Implement Salesforce Contracts

Plan your Salesforce Contracts implementation by understanding the product's capabilities, assembling the right team, and mapping your business requirements to features.

A successful implementation starts with preparation. Before you configure Salesforce Contracts, review the key capabilities to understand what the product offers, then assess how your organization's contracting processes — authoring, approvals, negotiations, execution, and obligation tracking — map to specific features. The data model overview helps you understand the objects and relationships that support those processes.

You also need the right people involved early. Identify stakeholders from sales, legal, operations, and IT, and define their roles in the implementation. Once your team is in place and your requirements are mapped, follow the step-by-step setup instructions to install, configure, and extend Salesforce Contracts for your organization.
