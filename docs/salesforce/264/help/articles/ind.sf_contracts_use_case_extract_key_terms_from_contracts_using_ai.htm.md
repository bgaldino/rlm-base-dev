# Use Case: Extract Key Terms from Contracts Using AI

As a Sales Representative reviewing an agreement with Cloud Kicks, use AI-powered contract tools to locate the MSA, extract renewal, pricing, and termination terms, ask clarifying questions, and share a summary with your team for internal alignment.

Before You Begin

Make sure that Agentforce for Contracts is configured in your org and that you have permission to access the Contract Search UI and contract records.

Goals
Quickly locate relevant contract documents using natural language search
Generate contract summaries to surface renewal, termination, and pricing terms
Ask clarifying questions to uncover obligations or red flags
Share a business-friendly summary via email for internal collaboration
Search Contracts

You’ve identified the customer and now need to locate their agreement. Use a natural language search to quickly retrieve the contract record.

On the contract list page, use the Search Contracts option.
In the search bar, enter a natural language prompt such as: “Pull up all agreements with Cloud Kicks where the delivery schedule is under 7 days and there are terms which protect against unforeseen circumstances.”
Select the most relevant result from the list.
Summarize a Contract

To avoid reading the full contract, you can instantly generate a summary highlighting the key business terms.

Summaries for the first two contract records are generated by default.
Use the Summarize this option to generate a summary for other records.

The summary highlights key information such as:

Parties involved and contract duration
Renewal and early termination conditions
Discount commitments and pricing structures
Other important clauses based on your query

Example summary output:

This agreement outlines a 2-year term with an automatic 1-year renewal, payment milestones tied to delivery schedules, a 30-day termination notice period, and a 10% volume-based discount for purchases exceeding 1,000 units. The agreement is governed by California law.

Ask Follow-Up Questions

Want to clarify specific sections? Use natural language to query the contract and get AI-generated answers.

Use the Ask Contract Questions option next to the relevant contract (when a contract agent is configured).
Ask questions in natural language. Examples:
What does the delivery clause state?
Is there a force majeure clause?
What are the timelines or penalties for late delivery?
Who is responsible for delivery failures?

Review AI-generated responses and take note of any action items or risks.

NOTE The AI answers based on contract language. It doesn’t offer legal interpretations or business advice.
Share the Insights via Email

Summarize your findings and align with internal stakeholders by sending the details via email.

If your org uses the Draft or Revise Email action, you can send the summary and key insights directly from the agent interface. This step is optional.
