# On-Time Renewals

An on-time renewal starts a new term immediately after the current term ends, without a gap between terms.

When the renewal uses catalog pricing, the renewal quote shows the applicable catalog rates and grant quantities. Previously negotiated base rates, tier rates, and grant quantities don’t automatically carry forward. To continue a customer’s negotiated terms, review the renewal quote and negotiate those values again before finalizing the quote.

Where rate and grant retention is available, setting the asset’s Pricing Source to Last Transaction carries forward the negotiated rates and original sold grant quantities instead. For details, see Retain Negotiated Rates and Grants on Renewal.

On-time renewal extends the effective end date of the existing usage resource policy record associated with the asset and usage resource to cover the renewed term. This extension doesn’t preserve previously negotiated rates or grant quantities on a catalog-priced renewal quote.
