# Effective Ways to Provide Context for Contracts AI Clause Generation

To enhance user experience and ensure accurate clause results, clause designers must consider specific parameters when entering their queries. The query elaboration is limited to a specific character count. Salesforce recommends implementing these parameters to streamline and improve the efficiency of the generative AI clause-drafting experience.

Some potential parameters for users include:

Valid query: Make sure your clause query is valid and coherent. It’s a complete and meaningful sentence. For example, outline a clause concerning the transfer of intellectual property after the termination of the contract.

Provide context: Offer enough context in your clause query so that the generative AI understands your clause request. If your query is too vague, generative AI sometimes provides inaccurate or irrelevant responses. Including prerequisites or explanatory context in queries leads to an enhancement in the quality of the output. For example, draft a nondisclosure agreement (NDA) for a technology company partnering with a software development company in California. This nondisclosure agreement is intended to protect confidential information shared during the partnership and is governed by the laws of the State of California.

Avoid bias and toxicity: Be cautious about the language and topics you introduce. Avoid discriminatory or offensive content, and refrain from promoting harmful stereotypes. The generative AI is designed to address and mitigate bias, toxicity, and other negative elements in its generated content. Examples:

Bias: An example of age bias is the belief that older employees are less productive than younger ones.

Toxicity: An example of hate speech includes offensive language and derogatory slurs directed at individuals or groups.

Negative stereotypes: An example of reinforcing stereotypes is the belief that all programmers are socially awkward.

Spelling and grammar: Check your input for spelling and grammar errors. While generative AI is proficient in understanding the English language, clear and error-free input leads to better clause results.

Avoid capitalizations: Use proper capitalization. Excessive capitalization can affect the quality of the response, as the generative AI sometimes interprets it as an acronym or emphasizes certain words unnecessarily.

Unwanted spaces: Ensure that no unnecessary spaces are in your query. Extra spaces can confuse the generative AI and affect the accuracy of the clause response.

Be clear and specific: Clearly state your clause request.

Use proper punctuation: Use punctuation marks appropriately. Commas, periods, and question marks help convey the intended meaning of your query.

Be patient: The generative AI sometimes takes a moment to generate clause responses, especially for complex queries. Allow the generative AI some time to provide a thoughtful answer.

Rephrase if necessary: If the initial clause response doesn’t meet your needs, consider rephrasing or providing additional context to refine the clause response.
