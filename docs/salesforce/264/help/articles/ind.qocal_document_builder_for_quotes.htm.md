# Document Builder for Quotes

Use Document Builder to design professional quote documents in Revenue Cloud.

REQUIRED EDITIONS
Available in: Lightning Experience
Available in: Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer Editions of Revenue Cloud where Transaction Management is enabled

Document Builder and its associated tools provide these capabilities for your quote generation process.

Customize standard quote document templates to align with your business practices.
Drag standard components into the builder, including Company Details, Grid, and Image components.
Show relevant information about a quote and its related records by using the Field panel.
Share details about completed work in real time.
Preview, generate, and email PDF documents to customers within seconds.
Capture digital signatures on the web and in the Salesforce mobile app when the template contains a signature component.
Choose between Document Builder and the existing quote PDF generation feature when you turn on Document Builder in Salesforce.
