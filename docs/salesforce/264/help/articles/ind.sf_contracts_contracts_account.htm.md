# Account Dashboard for Salesforce Contracts Analytics

Embed this dashboard on the Account page to get a quick view on the total revenue from all active contracts and analyze the revenue by each contract stage. You can also filter the contracts based on the days left before expiration to view the contracts due for renewal and their revenue.

Use the Contracts Account dashboard to view:

The total revenue generated from all active contracts.

The revenue distribution by each contract stage.

The contract stages that contribute the most to the overall revenue.

The contracts that are due for renewal based on the days left before expiration.

The revenue associated with contracts that require renewal.
