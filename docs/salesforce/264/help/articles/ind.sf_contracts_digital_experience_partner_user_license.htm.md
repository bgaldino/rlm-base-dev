# Generate Contract Documents On Digital Experience Partner Sites

Now you can generate contract documents and manage contract lifecycles on Digital Experience Partner sites.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, Professional, and Developer editions.

Who: This feature is available for users who are assigned the CLM Digital Experience Partner User permission set, and Contract Lifecycle Management for Digital Experience Partner User permission set license.
