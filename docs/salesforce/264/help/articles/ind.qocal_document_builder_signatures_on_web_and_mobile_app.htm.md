# Capture Signatures on Web and Mobile App

Add a digital signature to a quote PDF in the office or in the field. Signatures help make quote PDFs legally binding and professional. Capture signatures on the web or in the Salesforce mobile app.

REQUIRED EDITIONS
Available in: Lightning Experience
Available in: Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer Editions of Revenue Cloud where Quotes are enabled

Before a user can sign a document, make sure that the template is configured to support it. The signature component for quotes is only available in FSL enabled orgs. Follow the Document Builder set up steps to add a signature component to your template.

Go to a quote record and follow the Generate PDF Document steps.
In the edit preview mode, review your quote details.
Click the Add Signaturebutton. In the signature window, enter your Full Name.
Use either of the signature methods:
Text-Based: Select to have your typed name converted into a stylized digital signature.
Manual: Use your mouse (web) or finger or stylus (mobile app) to manually draw your signature.
Click Save. The signature is reflected within the document editor view.
Click Preview PDF to see the paginated view of the document with the signature positioned.
Click Create PDF to save the signed document to the quote’s related list.
