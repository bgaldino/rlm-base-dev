# Stack Renewals on Termed Assets with an Active Future-Dated Renewal

Renew a termed asset even when it has an active future-dated renewal. Each new renewal quote and order builds on the end date of the most recently created renewal, so you can stack multiple renewals for a single asset. Previously, when a future-dated renewal existed on an asset, sales reps waited for the renewal activation before renewing that asset again.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Growth or the Revenue Cloud Advanced license.

Why: With renewal stacking, you can:

Process each termed asset's next renewal the moment it's ready, instead of waiting for an existing future-dated renewal to activate. Your renewal desk and sales reps keep quotes and orders moving without manual workarounds.
Renew the same termed asset multiple times ahead of schedule, when a customer wants to lock in pricing early or align renewal terms across bundled assets. The renewal dates stay accurate no matter how many renewals you stack.

Renewal stacking applies only to termed assets and their regular asset bundles. We don't support renewal stacking for ramped assets, usage-based assets, or derived pricing plan assets.
