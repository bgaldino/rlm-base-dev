# Keep Quote Approvals Moving with Automated Workflows

Route quote discounts through approval levels based on your configured thresholds. Prebuilt workflows schedule reminders for pending work and escalate overdue approvals. These workflows help prevent approvals from stalling deals.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Advanced license.

How: In Salesforce Go, click Quote Approvals. Install Quote Smart Approval, Approval Follow-Up and Auto-Escalation, or both bundles in a sandbox.
