# Reuse Existing Simple Products as Bundles

Convert your existing simple products into bundles with a quick update to the product type. Previously, transitioning a simple product into a bundle required building a separate product record.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Growth, Revenue Cloud Advanced, or Revenue Cloud Billing license.

How: On the product record, change the product type to bundle.
