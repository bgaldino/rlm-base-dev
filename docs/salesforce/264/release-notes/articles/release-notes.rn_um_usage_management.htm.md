# Usage Management

Renew usage subscriptions at the right time to keep pace with your customers' changing needs. Start renewals early when the business expands, or restore a service after expiration to win back customers. Adjust rates and grant quantities for both standard and ramped pricing schedules while maintaining asset continuity.

Adapt Subscriptions When Customer Needs Change
When your customer's business grows or their requirements change, renew their active usage assets before the assets expire instead of canceling them and placing another order. Adjust rates and grant quantities to meet your customer's current needs while maintaining asset continuity.
Respond to Growth with Early Ramp Renewal
When customers with ramped pricing need to renew early, start the term at any point in their current schedule: year 1, year 2, or later. The renewal automatically extends coverage beyond the original end date, recreates subsequent segments with updated dates and the latest catalog rates.
Win Back Customers by Restoring Lapsed Subscriptions
Restore service when usage subscriptions expire and bring back customers with updated rates and grant quantities. Preserve the asset history for reporting continuity.
Prevent Usage Summary Failures by Updating Overridden Flows
Update your overridden Usage Management flows to use version 4 of the Data Processing Engine (DPE) definitions and prevent usage summary failures. Version 3 DPE definitions are no longer supported and can’t be opened, edited, or run.
New and Changed Objects in Usage Management
Review the fields that are removed in Usage Management and update your integrations to use the corresponding objects.
