# Find Products with Prefix Matching and Partial Search

Index and Search now support prefix matching for Product Name or Product Code, or Product SKU fields when marked as searchable. For more flexibility, partial search helps sales reps find products by typing any portion of Product Code or Product SKU, including the beginning, middle, or end, without needing exact formatting or delimiters.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Management, formerly Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Growth, Revenue Cloud Advanced, or Revenue Cloud Billing license.

How: Prefix search is enabled by default when Product Name, Product Code, or Product SKU fields are marked as searchable in Index and Search Configuration. To turn on partial search, from the Product Catalog Management home page, click Index and Search Configuration, and then click Index Settings. In the Search section, turn on partial search.
