# Adapt Subscriptions When Customer Needs Change

When your customer's business grows or their requirements change, renew their active usage assets before the assets expire instead of canceling them and placing another order. Adjust rates and grant quantities to meet your customer's current needs while maintaining asset continuity.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Management, formerly Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Advanced license.

Note: Early renewal isn't available in orgs that have both the Revenue Cloud Advanced and Revenue Cloud Billing licenses with the Usage Management add-on.

How: From an active usage asset, create a renewal quote and select Override Renewal Term. Select a start date before the current asset end date.
