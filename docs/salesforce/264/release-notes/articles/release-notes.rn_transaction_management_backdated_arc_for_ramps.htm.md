# Backdate Amendments, Renewals, and Cancellations for Ramp Deals to Adjust Billing

Sales reps can now set past effective dates on amendments, renewals, and cancellations for ramped assets. Previously, these transactions required effective dates of today or later, and teams had no way to correct billing for past periods in Salesforce.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Growth or the Revenue Cloud Advanced license.

Why: Multiyear ramp deals don't always get amended, renewed, or canceled on the exact day a contract change takes effect. When an issue causes a transaction delay past the intended date, sales reps can now set a past effective date to correct the record.

How: Create the amendment, renewal, or cancellation quote from the ramp asset record in Revenue Cloud. Set the effective date or renewal start date to any past date on or after the asset's lifecycle start date.
