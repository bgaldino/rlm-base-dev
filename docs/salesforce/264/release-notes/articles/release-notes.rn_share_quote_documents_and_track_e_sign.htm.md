# Share Quote Documents and Track E-Signatures from Salesforce

Share draft quote PDFs by email and manage quote documents for e-signature without leaving Salesforce. Your sales reps can generate a quote document from a template, send it through DocuSign, and track the envelope status on the quote. Your sales reps spend less time exporting, uploading, and checking signature status.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Advanced license.

Who: To design quote document templates, admins need the DocDesigner permission set.

How: In Salesforce Go, click Quote Negotiation and E-Signature. Install Draft Quote Sharing, Quote Document Generation and E-Signature, or both bundles in a sandbox.
