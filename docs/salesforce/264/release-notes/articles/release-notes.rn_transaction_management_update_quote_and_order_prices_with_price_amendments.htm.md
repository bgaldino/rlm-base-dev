# Gain Pricing Flexibility with Price Amendments

Your sales reps can now use price amendments to update the sales price on quote lines or the unit price on order lines without modifying quantities, attributes, or bundle configurations. Updated prices are reflected throughout the standard order and billing processes.

Where: This change applies to Lightning Experience in Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer editions of Revenue Management, formerly Revenue Cloud, with the Revenue Cloud Growth or the Revenue Cloud Advanced license.

How: Amend the asset, specify the effective date, enter the new sales price on the quote line or the new unit price on the order line, and save your changes.
