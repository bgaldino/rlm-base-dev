# Advanced Approvals Business APIs

Use the Advanced Approvals Business APIs to preview approval levels and related details before you submit a record for approval.

This table lists the available Advanced Approvals resources.

| Resource | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [`/connect/advanced-approvals/approval-submission/preview`](./connect-resources-preview-approvals.md) (POST) | Preview the approval levels of a record and associated level details, approval chains, approvers, and conditions before you submit the record for an approval. |
