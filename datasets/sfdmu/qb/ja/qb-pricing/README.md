# `qb/ja/qb-pricing` Data Plan

SFDMU data plan for the Japanese QuantumBit pricing: currency types, the standard price book and its entries, price adjustment schedules and tiers, attribute- and bundle-based adjustments, and the cost book. It is the `ja` counterpart of `qb/en-US/qb-pricing` for an org whose corporate currency is JPY. No CCI task loads it; run it with `sf sfdmu run` (or `LoadSFDMUData` with `pathtoexportjson`) after `qb/ja/qb-pcm`.

> **Structure follows en-US (todo 212).** Every object it shares with `qb/en-US/qb-pricing` has en-US's operation and externalId, so a reload matches on the key and nothing is deleted first. The data stays the ja set: fewer products (the ja catalog doesn't carry all of en-US's) and JPY prices. Bringing the catalog and its translations level with en-US is separate work.

## Org shape

- **JPY corporate.** `CurrencyType.csv` has JPY as the corporate currency (rate 1) and six others as JPY-per-unit rates. A JPY-corporate scratch org comes from a definition with `"country": "JP"`; this repo's is the `ent-sb0-jp` shape (`orgs/internal/ent-sb0-jp.json`).
- **Don't layer it with a USD shape.** qb/ja's schedules, adjustments and rates are in JPY (its price book carries USD entries alongside the JPY ones); loading it into an org built with qb, q3 or mfg would change their corporate currency's rates and schedules. `check_sfdmu_layering.py` lists the currency and date differences with mfg and q3 as known conflicts on that ground.

## Price adjustment schedules

- **`Upsert` on `Name`, as in en-US.** A JPY-corporate 264 org seeds the Attribute, Bundle and Price Adjustment Tier schedules in JPY, so the three rows match them; nothing is created. The CSV carries them in JPY, dated 2023-01-01 as en-US's are. "Standard Volume Based Adjustment", which the ja set used to carry, is gone: no procedure reads it.
- **The tiers hang off "Standard Price Adjustment Tier"**, the volume schedule `RLM_DefaultPricingProcedure` reads, as in en-US. The children (tiers, attribute- and bundle-based adjustments) reference the schedule by `PriceAdjustmentSchedule.Name`.

## Price book entries

- **Standard book only.** All entries are in the Standard Price Book, keyed `Product2.StockKeepingUnit;ProductSellingModel.Name;CurrencyIsoCode` as in en-US, and the target query is scoped `WHERE Pricebook2.IsStandard = true` so the match never sees another book's entry.

## Excluded

- **`PricebookEntryDerivedPrice`** stays `excluded: true`; it was excluded before todo 212, and its four rows (two USD, two JPY) have never been loaded or verified. en-US's own CSV has no rows. Including them is part of the catalog work above.
